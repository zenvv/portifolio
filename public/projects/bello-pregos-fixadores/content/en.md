### Bello Pregos & Fixadores

<p><em>logo for the new nails and fasteners brand from the Bello Aramados group</em></p>

## About

**Bello Pregos & Fixadores** is the new company under the **Bello Aramados** group, focused on importing and reselling nails, fasteners and nail guns. This is the logo developed for the brand's launch, made entirely in **Figma**.

## Concept

The logo follows a brutalist visual language: sharp corners, no curves, strongly geometric shapes. The central element is a nail piercing the "X", a direct reference to the company's segment and to the idea of fastening.

The name was condensed into the acronym **BFX** (**B**ello Pregos e **F**i**x**adores), used as a standalone icon in smaller applications, paired with the full "BELLO PREGOS & FIXADORES" wordmark in the extended versions.

The palette is lean: red as the icon's accent color, black/charcoal for the text, with white variants for use on dark backgrounds.
