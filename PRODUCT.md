# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers screening for full-time (CLT) software developer roles, scanning quickly and comparing candidates. Secondary: PJ/freelance clients evaluating Willian for contract automation/integration work. Bilingual audience (Brazilian Portuguese primary, English secondary).

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Willian Zeni (software developer / UI-UX designer) whose job is to get a recruiter to believe, within seconds of landing, that he solves real business problems, not just to list technologies. Success is a recruiter or client remembering a concrete solved problem and taking action (view projects, download CV, make contact).

## Positioning

The combination of full-stack app development (React/TypeScript) with enterprise process automation and systems integration (Microsoft Power Platform, Power BI, SharePoint/Graph API) inside real industrial/production environments: most candidates have one side of this, not both.

## Operating Context

- Bilingual site, PT-BR and EN, user-toggled.
- Dark/light theme toggle (class-based `.dark`), animated page transitions between routes (View Transitions API).
- Two live surfaces today: home (hero + featured work + contact + folded-in About) and a projects section with case-study detail pages.
- CV is downloadable as a file from the home page.
- Deployed on Vercel; stack is React 19 + Vite + Tailwind CSS v4 + shadcn/ui + react-router; fonts currently loaded: Bricolage Grotesque Variable (headings/sans), Inter Variable, Azeret Mono Variable.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content is data-driven (project cards, tech icons, experience/education timeline): the redesign works with existing content structures, not a rewrite of copy/data.
- Must keep working bilingual (PT-BR/EN) and dark/light theming.
- Confirmed brand constraint: keep yellow, black, white, gray as the palette; body font stays sans-serif (typeface itself is open).

## Brand Commitments

- Signature: "Hi, I'm Will :^)" personal/playful voice, confirmed as a real identity trait, but per user direction it should recede on the homepage rather than lead it (see Product Principles).
- Palette: yellow (accent), black, white, gray: binding, do not replace with a different hue.
- Body typography: sans-serif: binding; specific typeface family is open for the redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

- Three featured case studies with quantified outcomes, e.g. "6h → 15–30min" cycle-time reduction, "18 telas · 5 automações": real, usable proof points, not placeholders:
  - ERP: Bello Aramados (production/sales/finance/tax ERP, 6 areas, 7 SharePoint sites, ~18 screens generated by configuration).
  - Nailly (scheduling + finance PWA for a self-employed manicurist, batch agenda for 20–40 clients).
  - App (real-time industrial production tracking/operational control, FIFO pipeline, 30+ operators).
- Experience timeline: Bello Aramados (PJ, Dev & TI), Centro Tecnológico Randon (Data Intelligence Jr. Analyst, Administrative Assistant, Intern).
- Education: IFRS (Tecnologia em Análise e Desenvolvimento de Sistemas), Colégio Estadual São Tiago.
- Contact channels: GitHub, LinkedIn, email, WhatsApp.
- Personal-life material exists (photos, music list) but is explicitly being reduced/de-emphasized on the homepage per the current redesign brief: future work should not fabricate more of it, and should feel free to shrink or relocate what exists rather than treat it as untouchable.

## Product Principles

1. Recruiter-scan-speed over completeness: the homepage's job is to be understood and remembered in seconds, not to list everything Willian has done.
2. Lead with a solved problem and its number, not a skills inventory: the "6h → 15–30min" kind of proof is the hook, tech-stack icons are supporting evidence, not the headline.
3. Personal/playful identity (":^)", photos, music) is real and worth keeping somewhere, but recedes from the homepage; it does not compete with the professional pitch on first view.
4. One visual system across the whole site: the homepage redesign is the first surface, not a one-off; whatever direction wins must extend cleanly to the projects list, project case-study pages, and the About content.
5. Brand palette (yellow/black/white/gray) and a sans-serif body are non-negotiable; everything else (type choice, layout, density, motion) is open to a real redesign, not a tidy-up of the current one.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional product-specific requirement established beyond standard web accessibility (keep sufficient contrast for yellow-on-dark and yellow-on-light accent uses, which the current implementation already has to solve).
